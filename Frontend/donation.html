Our process

From lost to useful again.

01 / RECOVER

We wait

Found items stay in the Lost & Found system during the 30-day claim window.

02 / VERIFY

We check

Items are reviewed to make sure they are suitable for donation and no active claim remains.

03 / GIVE

We donate

Suitable items are sent to the campus charity drive, giving them a useful second life.
