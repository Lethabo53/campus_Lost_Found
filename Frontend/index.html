How the claim-ticket system works

1. Report

Log a lost or found item with a photo, category, and the exact campus spot.

2. Match

A keyword matching engine quietly ranks likely pairs the moment a new report comes in.

3. Verify

Claiming a found item requires answering two questions only the true owner would know, no contact details leak until verified.

4. Reunite

Verified claimants get the finder's contact details, and the finder earns Good Samaritan points on the leaderboard.
